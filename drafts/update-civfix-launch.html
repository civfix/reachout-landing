Reach Out launches civfix: the global volunteer hub

Report a problem in under a minute, see what your neighbors are flagging, and join or host a cleanup, all in one app on iPhone, Android and the web.

By Reach Out LA · Published September 25, 2026

Today, Reach Out Los Angeles is launching civfix, our resident reporting and volunteer hub. With civfix, reporting a problem and helping fix it happen in the same place, and you can report an issue or host a cleanup anywhere in the world.

See it, pin it, done

Every neighborhood has problems that fall through the cracks: an illegally dumped couch, a graffiti-covered wall, a pothole that’s been there for months, a streetlight that never comes back on. Reporting them usually means working out which department is responsible, digging up the right form or phone number, and then never hearing back. Most people give up, and the problem lingers.

With civfix, you snap a photo or a short video, the app tags the exact location, and you pick the type of issue: illegal dumping, graffiti, a pothole or broken sidewalk, a broken streetlight or flooding, overgrown brush, or something else. Reporting takes under a minute, and you don’t need to know who to call.

Sent to the right agency, and tracked

civfix works out which agency is responsible based on exactly where your pin is, forwards the report for you, and lets you track it and follow up. Every report gets a live status timeline, so you can see when it was submitted, forwarded, verified and scheduled for cleanup.

More than a complaint box

Plenty of neighbors would happily pitch in to clean things up; they just haven’t had an easy way to find each other or get organized. On civfix, anyone can host a cleanup. Neighbors can RSVP, see who’s going and what to bring, and coordinate in a group chat right in the app. You can also follow organizers and neighbors, message people directly, and keep up with a feed of what’s happening nearby.

Why we built it

civfix grew out of Reach Out’s work on the ground in Los Angeles, and a $10,000 donation earlier this year helped us build it. The immediate goal is simple: fewer potholes, faster responses, and less friction between a problem and its fix.

The bigger goal is a civic infrastructure where no issue goes unheard and every resident, regardless of their ZIP code or their familiarity with bureaucracy, has an equal and effective voice in the condition of their community. Right now, the loudest and most connected neighborhoods tend to get the most attention. civfix is built to change that.

Get civfix

Download the app for reporting on the spot, or use it in any web browser.
